## Phase 14: Runtime themes

- A test holds each switched screen equal to a fresh start. It passed at once: the old restyle path missed no pane or overlay.
- The stale parts were the text boxes, which used the colors of bubbles, and the cells that nothing draws on. The text boxes now follow the theme, and OSC 10/11 sets the colors of the terminal (`theme_paint`).
- The bubbles text area draws through a pointer that only Focus and Blur set. After a copy of the model, a style change did not show until the next Focus. A preview does not move the focus, so `input.SetStyles` sets the pointer again.
- Lesson: a test that passes on its first run must prove that it can fail. The equivalence test missed the pointer bug, because a pick moves the focus. The preview test catches it.
- `Ctrl+T`, a preview with an undo, `/theme <name>`, `/theme reload` and the file watch (`theme_watch`) are in. ADR-0016 records them.
- ISSUE-020 is closed.
